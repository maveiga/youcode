🎨 Teste de Filtros - Spline Branco & Azul

1. Invertido (Branco puro)

filter: invert(1) brightness(1.1);

2. Invertido + Azul

filter: invert(1) sepia(1) hue-rotate(190deg);

3. Claro + Azul suave

filter: brightness(1.6) hue-rotate(190deg);

4. Branco total

filter: brightness(2) saturate(0);

5. Azul claro intenso

filter: invert(1) sepia(0.5) saturate(1.5);

6. Gradiente Azul/Branco

filter: invert(1) brightness(1.25);
